Quick start

1. Pick the vehicle - select it from the pool of 16 before uploading anything.

2. Upload the monthly reports - Daily Movement Report and Vehicle Analysis Report for that vehicle, as soon as fleet sends them.

3. Capture each slip - fuel or toll. Let the AI read it, then check the fields.

4. Type the driver, manually - no shortcuts, no saved list. Write who was actually driving.

5. Search the vote line - only this field is searchable, from the WildOceans vote list.

6. Sync - pushes the vehicle's slips into its Google Sheets tab for that month, sorted by date.

Deadlines

Monthly fuel card and e-tag reconciliations, plus supporting slips, are due to finance/fleet by the 5th of every month.

Original slips and recons must still be batched and handed to finance/fleet after electronic submission, for audit and safekeeping.

Slips

Fuel only on the card dedicated to that specific vehicle. Every slip must be reconciled with the fuel card statement and submitted with its vote number and manager approval.

Only legible slips are accepted. Faded slips will be rejected - it's the driver's responsibility to keep them readable.

Missing slips get deducted from the driver's salary. You have up to 45 days to submit a missing or rejected slip before that recovery happens.

E-tags

All Trust vehicles now carry vehicle-specific e-tags for toll payment. Don't tamper with or remove one for any reason unless fleet or finance says so.

If an e-tag fails, the driver falls back to a physical toll slip - that's what the Toll slip type in this app is for.

Basics

Trust vehicles are for work purposes only. Zero tolerance on traffic law violations - can lead to a disciplinary hearing or dismissal.

Each driver gets a Blue Driver Identity Tag, uniquely coded. Never swap or lend your tag to another driver.

When vehicles are shared between drivers, an audit sheet must be completed on collection and return.

Night driving and fatigue

Night driving is prohibited without written manager permission (roughly 6pm-6am winter, 7pm-5am summer). Emergencies still require notifying your manager immediately.

Long-distance trips need pre-planned driver rotation and rest stops to manage fatigue.

Fraud

Claiming personal-vehicle mileage for a trip actually done in a Trust vehicle, or claiming travel you weren't present for, is fraud - can mean termination.

Accidents and incidents

Any accident, however minor, must be reported to SAPS for a case number and to the fleet department immediately. A signed Accident Report is due within 24 hours.

Each vehicle has a roadside assist QR code on the windscreen - scan it for step-by-step help after a breakdown or incident.

Changed in the 16 March 2026 policy

Hire booking window shortened - was 5 days before pickup, now 2-3 days.

E-tags replace magnetic toll cards - vehicle-specific, installed fleet-wide.

Dash cams formalised - inside and outside coverage, cloud-stored, usable for investigations, safety monitoring, training or legal purposes.

Roadside assistance QR codes added to every windscreen.

Non-staff drivers now need ID copy, licence copy, proof of residence, indemnity form and signed asset use agreement.

Hired vehicle checklists - check-in and check-out inspection forms now required.

Fraud clarified - personal-mileage claims for Trust-vehicle trips, and absent-driver claims, explicitly called out as fraud.

Stronger road safety provisions - night driving restrictions, fatigue management on long trips, tighter tracking and dash cam monitoring.

Non-staff passengers restricted - only trustees, donors, visitors, research students or similar, with a signed indemnity form.

Which slips go into this app?

Fuel slips mainly, since e-tags now cover tolls. If an e-tag fails, submit the physical toll slip instead and mark it as type Toll.

Why can't I search for the driver's name?

On purpose. Someone has to actually type who was driving, every time, rather than pick the last name off a list. Keeps the entry honest.

Where does the vote list come from?

The same WildOceans vote listing ReconEasy uses - one project per tab, vote number and description per line.

What if I don't know the vote number?

Check with your project manager before saving the slip. Don't guess - fuel and toll reconciliations with the wrong vote get rejected by finance.

What happens when I tap Sync?

Every slip captured for that vehicle gets written into its Google Sheets tab for the month, sorted by date from the 1st to month end - even if you uploaded reports out of order.

Do I still need the paper slip after capturing it?

Yes. The policy still requires original slips to reach finance for audit and safekeeping - this app doesn't replace that step.

What if I miss the 5th of the month deadline?

You get up to 45 days to submit a missing or rejected slip before it's recovered from your salary - but don't wait, submit as soon as you have it.
